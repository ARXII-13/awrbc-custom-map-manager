# Credits

## Artwork

**Toen's Medieval Strategy Sprite Pack (16x16)** by **Andre Mari Coppola**, used
for terrain in the map editor and shipped in `web/assets/toen/`.

- Licence: [CC BY 4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Source: <https://opengameart.org/content/toens-medieval-strategy-sprite-pack-v10-16x16>
- Author: <https://toen.itch.io/toens-medieval-strategy>

Changes made: a subset of the tileset is referenced by
`web/assets/toen/manifest.json`; the image itself is unmodified.

Everything else drawn by the project - the procedural icons in `web/icons.js`,
and all terrain, army and unit colours - is original.

## Not included

No assets from Advance Wars, on any platform, are in this repository. Sprites
from those games are Nintendo and Intelligent Systems copyright, and shipping
them is what would put the archive and website at risk. `web/sprites/` exists
for anyone who wants to use other art locally; it is gitignored and never
published. See `web/sprites/README.md` and decision #37.
